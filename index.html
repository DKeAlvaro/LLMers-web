Dutch, from your first hallo to A2+.

63 lessons that build on each other, native audio for every word, and answers you write yourself, checked by Jev. Free, in your browser, on any phone or laptop.

Start learning How a lesson works

One lesson, five stops

  1. Words

    Meet 20–30 new words and phrases, each with native audio and an example.

  2. Grammar

    One short note at a time, with tables and spoken examples.

  3. Dialogue

    See everything in a real scene: a café, the doctor, the gemeente.

  4. Practice

    About 20 exercises, from matching to writing whole sentences.

  5. Talk

    A roleplay where you answer freely and get corrected on the spot.

Corrections that actually explain

Type your own Dutch. Jev, a typed classifier, decides if it is right, spots a typo or the exact word that is wrong, and picks which common mistake you made, so the explanation you see was written for that exercise. No chatbot guessing at your grammar.

Teach before test

You start knowing nothing, so every word you read, hear or have to write was taught first. We check this mechanically for all 63 lessons: no surprise vocabulary, ever.
